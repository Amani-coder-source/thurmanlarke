HOW WE HANDLE YOUR PERSONAL DATA

We deal in trust before we deal in advice. This notice explains, plainly, what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

§ 1

Who We Are

ThurmanLarke is a corporate advisory and regulatory compliance consultancy. We advise businesses, founders, and institutions on corporate governance, M&A transactions, regulatory strategy, data protection, and commercial legal matters.

We are the data controller in respect of personal data processed through this website and in connection with our services. References to "ThurmanLarke", "we", "us", or "our" in this notice refer to the firm.

OUR COMMITMENT

We process only what is necessary, retain it only for as long as the law or our relationship requires, and handle it with the same rigour we apply to every client matter.

§ 3

Our Lawful Basis for Processing

Every processing activity rests on a documented lawful basis under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) issued by the NDPC. We rely on:

01

Performance of a Contract

When you engage our services, we process your data to deliver on your retainer or mandate — including drafting documents, conducting due diligence, and providing legal advice.

02

Legitimate Interests

We process technical and usage data to maintain website security, improve our services, and manage client relationships. We have conducted a Legitimate Interest Assessment and determined our interests do not override your rights.

03

Consent

For marketing communications and our regulatory intelligence updates, we rely on your freely given, informed, specific, and unambiguous consent. You may withdraw it at any time — this does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

04

Legal Obligation

Certain data is retained to comply with Nigerian tax, regulatory, and anti-money laundering obligations, including requirements under NRA and the CBN.

§ 4

Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your data. We do not trade in it. Sharing occurs only where operationally necessary or legally required.

Recipient Category Purpose Basis
IT & Hosting Providers Website operation, cloud storage, email infrastructure Legitimate interests; contractual necessity
Payment Processors Invoice settlement (e.g. Paystack, bank transfer) Contract performance
Professional Advisers External counsel, auditors (bound by confidentiality) Legitimate interests; legal obligation
Regulatory Bodies CAC, NRA, CBN, NDPC and other competent authorities Legal obligation

All third-party processors are vetted for NDPA compliance and bound by appropriate data processing agreements.

§ 5

Cross-Border Data Transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside Nigeria. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we do so only where the destination country provides an adequate level of data protection, or where appropriate safeguards such as contractual clauses approved by the NDPC are in place.

To request details of the specific transfer mechanisms applicable to your data, contact our privacy desk.

§ 6

Your Rights Under the NDPA 2023

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 affords you substantive rights over your personal data.

Right What It Means
Right to Access Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Right to Rectification Require us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
Right to Erasure Request deletion where processing is no longer justified.
Right to Object Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
Right to Restriction Require us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
Right to Data Portability Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
Withdraw Consent Withdraw consent for marketing or discretionary processing at any time.
Right to Complain Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).
EXERCISING YOUR RIGHTS

To exercise any right, write to privacy@thurmanlarke.com. We respond within 30 days in line with the NDPA and GAID timelines, with a possible 60-day extension where the request is complex or numerous.

§ 8

Security Measures

We deploy layered organisational, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and periodic security reviews.

In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware and, where required, will notify affected data subjects without undue delay.

§ 9

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses only strictly necessary cookies required for basic site functionality.

We do not place advertising, profiling, or third-party tracking cookies without your explicit consent.

Where we deploy analytics tools, we configure them in privacy-preserving mode.

You can control cookie preferences via your browser settings at any time.

§ 10

Changes to This Notice

We review this notice periodically and whenever changes in our processing activities or applicable law require an update.

The version number and date at the top of this document reflect the most recent revision.

Material changes will be communicated directly to active clients.
